    Twenty-seven years of new schools.

    Every charter open today, by the year it opened. Schools that closed aren't in the directory, so older years look smaller than they really were.

    How it works

    Three steps. No spreadsheets.

    01
    Pick your lens

    Parent, teacher, student or funder. Each lens sorts the schools and picks the facts up front.

    02
    Wander the map →

    Filter by borough, authorizer or network. Find what's near you. Tap any dot for the short version.

    03
    Heart, share, follow up

    Save a shortlist and send the link to whoever you're deciding with. Every profile says what to check next.
